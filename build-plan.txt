LAPSEPI – BUILD PLAN

END GOAL: v1
A simple travel timelapse device that:
- lets me prove the core flow with mocks first
- lets me take a photo and see it in a basic web view
- lets me frame the shot before starting a run
- stores images cleanly by session
- turns them into a video
- lets me get that video onto my phone
- (later) has power management baked in
