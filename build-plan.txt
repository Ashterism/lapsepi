LAPSEPI – BUILD PLAN

END GOAL: v1
A simple travel timelapse device that:
- lets me prove the core flow with mocks first
- lets me take a photo and see it in a basic web view
- lets me frame the shot before starting a run
- stores images cleanly by session
- lets me review captured sessions in the browser
- lets me create a video from a chosen session
- lets me get that video onto my phone
- (later) has power management baked in

## CURRENT SHAPE / NOTES
- Home page is for taking a test photo, starting a timelapse, stopping a timelapse, and checking simple current status.
- Gallery should be its own page.
- Create-video should be its own page.
- Full session details can wait until V2.
- This needs to behave like a small appliance, not just a Python app.
- Boot, dirty shutdown recovery, and reliable restart are part of getting to v1.
- A stale lockfile must never leave the device unusable after power loss.
- Phone access must still work when there is no external Wi-Fi available.
- Known Wi-Fi is useful for updates, admin, and easier access when networks exist.
- Connectivity should be handled simply and robustly; clever switching can come later.
- Hardware controls like LED and off button can come later, once software recovery is solid.
